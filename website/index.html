Questions before you book.

What logistics managers ask us before the first trip.

Which container sizes do you move?

20 ft, 40 ft and 40 ft high cube containers, between Jeddah Islamic Port, dry ports, yards, factories and distribution centres.

Do you deliver outside Jeddah?

Yes. We run from Jeddah to all 13 regions of Saudi Arabia, including Riyadh, Dammam, Madinah, Tabuk and Abha. We are domestic only and do not cross borders.

What can go on your flatbeds?

Steel, rebar, coils, sections, machinery and project cargo, secured with chains, straps and dunnage to suit the load, and delivered to building sites, plants and project sites.

How do I get a price?

Send the pickup point, destination, cargo and date by phone, WhatsApp or the form on this page. You get a written quotation with the truck, the rate and what it includes.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Transport General Authority licence 63/00063222 for heavy freight transport of commercial goods, valid until 15 September 2029. Commercial registration 7052479644, VAT number 314382023100003.

What paperwork do I get?

A signed proof of delivery and a ZATCA-compliant VAT invoice. Before loading you also get the plate number, driver name and driver phone.

Can I follow the shipment?

Yes. Your load has a dedicated driver, the shipment is tracked live, and someone in the Jeddah office answers your call until delivery.
